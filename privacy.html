Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how Etsoc LLC ("Etsoc", "we") handles information in running its e-commerce business.

Information we collect

  • Orders: name, phone number, delivery address and order details you give us when you buy from one of our online stores.
  • Communication: messages you send us by email, WhatsApp or social media.
  • Advertising performance: aggregated reports from advertising platforms (such as TikTok, Meta and Google) about our own ad accounts, for example spend, impressions, clicks and conversions.

How we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your orders.
  • To measure how our own advertising performs and decide where to spend our budget.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

Advertising platform data

We connect to advertising platforms' APIs with read-only access to our own ad accounts. We do not access other advertisers' accounts or platform users' personal profiles. This data is stored on secured internal systems, is used only by our internal team, and is never sold or shared with third parties.

Sharing

We share order details only with the courier partners who deliver your order and with service providers that host our store and systems. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Access is limited to authorised team members, and credentials and access tokens are stored securely.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing info@etsoc-group.com.
